# R6 application identification assets

These assets are used solely to identify the corresponding third-party
applications in One Memory. They are not One Memory branding, and their names
and marks remain the property of their respective owners.

- `workbuddy.png`, `codebuddy.png`, `cursor.png`, `claude.png`, `codex.png`:
  approved product-identification exports.
- `tianshu.png`: the 天枢 (Rivet) app's own `Contents/Resources/icon.icns`,
  exported at 256×256, so the app list and the detail page match the Dock.
- `deepseek.svg`, `hermes.svg`, `openclaw.svg`, `opencode.svg`, `pi.svg`,
  `qwen.svg`: application-identification artwork retained for compatibility
  with the supported source list.

Use is nominative and limited to identifying compatible products. This record
does not grant trademark rights or assert that third-party marks are licensed
under Apache-2.0. Replace or remove an asset if its owner requests it.
